Systems, data products, and strategic ambiguity

Because complexity isn't the enemy. Confusion is.

Hi, I'm Saurabh. I build tools for the space between data and decisions. I am useful in the gray area between "we know this matters" and "we know exactly how and what to build": the data is inconsistent, the workflow is fragile, ownership is unclear, and requirements keep changing as people learn what they actually need. My work turns raw operational data into usable decision systems. I've built ingestion pipelines, validation frameworks, QA harnesses, dashboards, automation tools, and internal platforms across utilities, finance, telecom, healthcare, research, and small business operations. Reach out if you need someone who can sit inside ambiguity, make the moving parts legible, and turn the mess into working software.
